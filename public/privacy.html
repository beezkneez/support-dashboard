Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Kronara provides scheduling, booking, membership and marketing software to fitness and movement studios. Each studio runs its own copy of the software and is responsible for its own members' and staff's information. This policy describes what Kronara handles when a studio's administrator connects the software to Facebook and Instagram advertising (Meta), and how we handle information generally.

1. What we receive from Meta

When a studio administrator chooses "Connect Facebook Ads" and approves access, we receive only what that administrator approves:

  • The names and IDs of the ad accounts and Facebook Pages the administrator can access.
  • The text, pictures and dates of the studio's own public Page posts, so the administrator can choose one to promote as an ad.
  • Advertising spend by day for the studio's ad account, so the studio can see what its advertising costs next to its revenue.
  • An access token that lets the software act for the administrator, valid for about 60 days.

We do not receive, read or store information about the people who see or click the studio's ads, and we do not access anyone's personal Facebook profile, friends, messages or private posts.

2. What we do with it

  • Create the ad campaigns, ad sets and ads that the studio's administrator reviews and asks us to create (always created paused).
  • Upload the pictures and videos the administrator chooses to Meta's advertising library.
  • Show the studio its advertising spend alongside its other business figures.

We use this information only to provide those features to the studio that connected. We do not sell it, use it for our own advertising, combine it with other studios' data, or use it to train AI models.

3. Where it is kept and who can see it

The access token is stored encrypted in the studio's own database. It is never sent to a web browser. Only that studio's administrators and staff members with marketing access can use the connection, and only within their own studio. Hosting and infrastructure providers process the data on our behalf under their own security commitments.

4. Retention and deletion

We keep the connection until the administrator disconnects it. Disconnecting immediately revokes the token with Meta and deletes the stored token, ad account list and Page list. Saved ad drafts remain until the studio deletes them and contain the wording and links the studio chose, plus the text of any Page post it picked. See how to delete your data.

5. Meta's platform terms

Our use of information received from Meta complies with Meta's Platform Terms and Developer Policies. You can review and remove the app's access at any time in Facebook under Settings & privacy, Settings, Business Integrations.

6. Security

We use encrypted connections, encrypt access tokens at rest, require authentication for every request that touches the connection, and limit who inside a studio can use it.

7. Children

The software is for studio staff and is not directed to children under 13. Ads created through it must not target people under 18.

8. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with a new date.

9. Contact

Questions or requests about this policy: use the support form inside your studio's app, or the support site at support.kronara.app.
