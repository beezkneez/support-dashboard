Delete Your Facebook Ads Data

Last updated: October 7, 2026

If you connected Facebook Ads to a Kronara studio, you can remove everything we hold from Facebook yourself, immediately, in either of two ways.

Option 1: Disconnect in the studio's app (recommended)

  1. Sign in to your studio's app as an administrator.
  2. Open Marketing → Settings.
  3. In Facebook Ads connection, press Disconnect and confirm.

This revokes our access with Facebook and deletes the stored access token and the list of ad accounts and Pages. It takes effect immediately.

Option 2: Remove the app in Facebook

  1. In Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations.
  2. Find the Kronara app and choose Remove.

Facebook stops honouring our access straight away. The studio's stored connection then stops working, and its administrator can disconnect it as in Option 1 to clear the stored details.

Saved drafts

Ad drafts a studio saved contain the wording and links the studio chose, and the text of any Page post it picked to promote. A studio administrator can delete any draft in Marketing → Facebook Ads. To have all of a studio's drafts and data removed, an administrator can ask through the support form in the app or at support.kronara.app. We confirm the request and complete it within 30 days.

What we never held

We do not collect information about the people who view or click a studio's ads, so there is nothing of theirs to delete. See our Privacy Policy.
